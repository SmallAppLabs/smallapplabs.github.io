TouchBlocker Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

TouchBlocker is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Overview

TouchBlocker ("the App") is an Android application that allows users to create an overlay on their screen to block touch inputs in specific areas. This privacy policy explains how the App handles user information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App operates entirely on your device and does not transmit any data to external servers.

Permissions Used

The App requires the following permissions to function:

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to draw the touch-blocking overlay on your screen. This permission is used solely for the App's core functionality and does not access or collect any data from other applications.
  • Foreground Service: Required to maintain the overlay service while the App is running in the background. This ensures the touch-blocking functionality remains active.

These permissions are used exclusively for the App's intended functionality and do not access any personal or sensitive data.

In-App Purchases

The App offers premium features through Google Play's billing system. When you make a purchase:

  • All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play
  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information
  • Your purchase status is stored locally on your device

Data Storage

All App settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not use cloud storage or remote servers. Your configuration data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases. Google's privacy policy applies to payment processing. You can review it at https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of collecting children's information.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no data to secure on our end. Your App settings are stored locally on your device and are protected by your device's security measures.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
